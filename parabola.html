Approaching a limit

Move the slider toward 0. The blue point comes from the left, and the orange point comes from the right.

f(x) = x²

Your browser does not support canvas.

Left: x = 1.50, f(x) = 2.2500

Right: x = 2.50, f(x) = 6.2500

What do you notice?

Both points move toward (2, 4). Their y-values get closer to 4, so the limit is 4.

lim (x → 2) x² = 4
