Infinite limits: practice questions

Decide whether the function goes toward +∞, −∞ or has different behavior on the two sides.

1. Approaching from the left

lim (x → 3⁻) 1 / (x − 3) = ?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: −∞. From the left, x is slightly less than 3, so x − 3 is a very small negative number. Dividing 1 by it gives increasingly large negative values.

2. A negative numerator

lim (x → 1⁺) −1 / (x − 1) = ?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: −∞. From the right, x − 1 is a very small positive number. The numerator is negative, so the quotient decreases without bound.

3. Both sides going up

lim (x → 3) 1 / (x − 3)² = ?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: +∞. Squaring makes the denominator positive on both sides of 3. It approaches zero from above, so the function grows without bound from both directions.

4. Find the vertical asymptote

Consider f(x) = 2 / (x + 4). Where is the vertical asymptote? Does the two-sided limit there exist?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: x = −4; the two-sided limit does not exist. The denominator approaches zero at −4. From the left the quotient goes toward −∞, and from the right it goes toward +∞.
