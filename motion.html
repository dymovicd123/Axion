Motion lab

Our car follows s(t) = t². Move through time and see the difference between distance travelled and speed at that moment.

Speed formulav(t) = 2t

What does this show?

As the car moves, its distance increases, but so does its speed. The derivative v(t) = 2t tells us how fast it is moving at each moment.

Compare the average speed from the start with the instantaneous speed. They are not the same: the car is speeding up as time goes on.
