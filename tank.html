Water tank lab

Water enters a tank over time. Change the flow rate and see how much water has accumulated.

Filling the tank

Current flow rate3.0 L/s

Total water0.0 L

Tank capacity60 L

V(t) = 3t

What does this show?

The flow rate tells us how many litres enter each second. The integral tells us the total amount of water added since the beginning.

Try setting the time to 4 seconds in both modes. With constant flow, 12 litres have entered. With increasing flow, the total is also 12 litres at that moment, but the rates are different. After that, the totals begin to separate.
