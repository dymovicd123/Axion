Introduction: practice questions

Try to work out what each function approaches before you open the answer.

1. A simple limit

What value does x² approach as x gets closer to 3?

lim (x → 3) x² = ?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 9. Values close to 3 have squares close to 9. For example, 2.9² = 8.41 and 3.1² = 9.61. Both sides approach 9.

2. Coming from two directions

Find the limit of f(x) = 2x + 1 as x approaches 1. Do the left and right sides agree?

lim (x → 1) (2x + 1) = ?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3. When x is just below 1, f(x) is just below 3. When x is just above 1, f(x) is just above 3. The two sides approach the same value.

3. A changed value at one point

Suppose f(x) = x² for every x except 2, but f(2) = 10. What is the limit as x approaches 2?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 4. Changing the value at exactly x = 2 does not change what happens close to 2. On both sides, x² still approaches 4.

4. Different left and right limits

A function approaches 1 from the left and 3 from the right as x approaches 0. Does its two-sided limit exist?

Show answer and explanation

Answer: No. A two-sided limit exists only when both sides approach the same value. Here one side approaches 1 and the other approaches 3.
