Approaching an asymptote

Move closer to x = 2. Notice that the values on the left and right go in opposite directions.

f(x) = 1 / (x − 2)

Your browser does not support canvas.

Left: x = 1.50, f(x) = -2.00

Right: x = 2.50, f(x) = 2.00

What do you notice?

The left side falls toward −∞, while the right side rises toward +∞. Since they do not approach the same value, the two-sided limit at x = 2 does not exist.
